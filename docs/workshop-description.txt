[h1]Improvised Still[/h1]

Craft a still from a cooking pot and a pipe, then turn any water into clean drinking water, or fruit and potatoes into moonshine. Works with vanilla DayZ; no other mods required.

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Craft:[/b] combine a cooking pot with a pipe. The still goes into your hands with the pipe attached.
[*][b]Collect:[/b] attach a glass bottle, plastic water bottle, canteen, water pouch or filtering bottle to the Collection Bottle slot.
[*][b]Heat:[/b] put it on a lit fireplace, fire barrel or portable gas stove.
[*][b]Clean water:[/b] fill it with any water (sea, pond, river, snow). Distilling removes all disease, so it beats boiling. Once bottled it looks like any other water, so remember which bottles you filled.
[*][b]Moonshine:[/b] fresh water plus fruit or potatoes, left unheated, ferments into mash (15 minutes by default), then distills into moonshine. Salt water will not ferment. Dried and cooked fruit work like fresh; rotten fruit gives half the moonshine and is more likely to go bad; burnt fruit will not ferment. Berries and pumpkin count as fruit; tomatoes, peppers and zucchini give a quarter of the moonshine. Fermenting slows in the cold and stops near freezing, and hot mash must cool first. Fruit added to ready mash needs half the time to ferment. Ready mash left unheated for 2 hours spoils.
[*][b]Heating up:[/b] moonshine starts coming over at 78 °C, clean water at 100 °C, so a full still takes a while to get going.
[*][b]Thermometer:[/b] attach one for 25% more moonshine.
[/list]

[h2]Seawater is dangerous[/h2]
Drinking seawater dehydrates you fast, makes you vomit, and causes saltwater sickness. Antibiotics will not help; a full saline IV cures it. Any water container can be filled from the sea, so you can haul seawater to your still.

[h2]Moonshine and mash[/h2]
[list]
[*]Moonshine disinfects wounds, rags and clothing like alcohol tincture, and washes bloody hands.
[*]Drinking gets you drunk: blur, stumbling, a warm feeling and pain relief, then vomiting and blackout if you overdo it. Food slows it down. It is saved with your character.
[*]Ready mash drinks as beer, but a batch can go bad and give food poisoning.
[*]The still also cooks food like a pot; boiled food does not burn while it holds liquid.
[*]Distilled clean water works as car coolant.
[/list]

[h2]Warning[/h2]
Blacking out is vanilla unconsciousness: if you disconnect or your game crashes while blacked out, you die. A clean server restart is safe.

[h2]For server owners[/h2]
Copy the key from the mod's keys folder to your server's keys folder, and add the mod's improvisedstill_types.xml to your mission: register it in a CE folder in cfgeconomycore.xml (e.g. customtypes), or copy its entry into db/types.xml. The still is crafted only; the entry sets how long an untouched still lasts (7 days by default). On first start the server creates profiles/ImprovisedStill/config.json, where you can tune fermentation time, saltwater sickness, drinking effects and more, or rename the moonshine (e.g. to vodka). Restart the server to apply changes.
